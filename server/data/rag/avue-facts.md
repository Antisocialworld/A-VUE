## CHUNK avue-refunds#policy
type: policy | source: avue-refunds | slug: policy | words: 48 | updated: 2026-11-19
If a showtime is cancelled, paid bookings are refunded. If your payment arrives after your seat hold expired and the seats were taken, a refund is processed automatically and you are emailed. Tickets cannot be cancelled in the app. For any other refund request, contact A VUE support.

## CHUNK avue-age#ratings
type: policy | source: avue-age | slug: ratings | words: 33 | updated: 2026-11-19
A VUE does not show age ratings in the app. The score shown on movies is an audience score out of 10, not an age rating. For age restrictions, contact A VUE support.

## CHUNK avue-entry#qr

Your ticket has a QR code. Show it at the cinema entrance. Opening or refreshing the ticket creates a new QR code and the old one stops working. A ticket that has already been used shows as used.
